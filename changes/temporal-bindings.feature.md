- Add `Temporal` bindings to the standard library, covering durations, instants, plain and zoned dates and times, and `Temporal.Now`, plus `Date.toTemporalInstant`. They need a runtime that provides Temporal, such as Node.js 26. Compare Temporal values with the `equals` and `compare` functions of their module, since `==`, `compare`, `<` and `>` treat any two of them as equal. GenType maps Temporal types to TypeScript's `Temporal.*` types, which need TypeScript 6.0 or later and its `esnext.temporal` lib (included in `ESNext`).
